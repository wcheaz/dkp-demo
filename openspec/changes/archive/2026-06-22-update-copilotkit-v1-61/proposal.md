## Why

Upgrading `@copilotkit/*` dependencies from version `1.60.2` to version `1.61.0` to take advantage of latest features, bug fixes, and performance improvements in the CopilotKit library.

## What Changes

- Update `@copilotkit/react-core` to `1.61.0`
- Update `@copilotkit/react-ui` to `1.61.0`
- Update `@copilotkit/react-textarea` to `1.61.0`
- Update `@copilotkit/runtime` to `1.61.0`
- Ensure the application builds and compiles successfully under the upgraded packages.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `upgrade-dependencies-v1-61`: Upgrade CopilotKit packages to 1.61.0.

### Modified Capabilities

None

## Impact

- `package.json` and `package-lock.json`
